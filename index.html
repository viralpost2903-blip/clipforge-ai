Frequently asked questions

Is ClipForge AI free right now?

This demo is free to publish and try. It does not include a paid AI backend, and AI model usage may cost money once connected.

Does the AI Video Generator create actual videos?

Not yet. It prepares a detailed prompt. Actual video generation requires connecting a video model through a backend service.

Does the text generator use a real AI model?

This initial version uses templates in your browser. A real AI text API can be connected in a later stage.

Are the subscription buttons connected to payments?

No. They are placeholders. Secure account management and a payment provider must be integrated before selling subscriptions.

Will my uploaded video be sent to a server?

The clipper in this version works in your browser. Your selected file is not uploaded by this code. Export support varies by browser.
